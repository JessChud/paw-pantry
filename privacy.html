Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Paw Pantry ("we", "us") is a pet replenishment assistant that helps you track your pets' food, treats, and supplies, and reminds you before you run out. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, provided when you sign up or contact us.
  • Pet profiles: the details you give us about your pets — name, species, breed, age, weight, diet, allergies, and product preferences. This is the core of the service; without it we can't predict when supplies run out.
  • Usage information: which products you view or ask about, reminder settings, and how you interact with the assistant, so we can improve recommendations.

What we don't collect: payment information. Purchases are completed on the retailer's own website (e.g. Chewy, Amazon) — we never see or handle your payment details.

2. How we use your information

  • To maintain your pet profiles and calculate when supplies will run out.
  • To send reminders you've asked for (e.g. "Biscuit's treats run out in 5 days").
  • To recommend products suited to your pets, including comparing prices across retailers.
  • To improve the service and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Affiliate links and what retailers see

Paw Pantry earns a commission when you buy through the purchase links we show — this is how the service stays free. When you click a purchase link, the retailer (e.g. Chewy, Amazon) receives standard referral information: that the visit came from Paw Pantry, and what you purchase. Their use of that information is governed by their privacy policies, not ours. We disclose the affiliate relationship in the assistant before any purchase link is shown.

4. Sharing

Apart from the affiliate referrals described above, we share your information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the service (e.g. hosting), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: view or update your pet profiles and account details at any time through the assistant or by emailing us.
  • Deletion: email hellopawpantry@gmail.com and we will delete your account and pet profiles within 30 days.
  • Reminders: turn reminders off at any time; we keep your profiles but stop contacting you.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, and delete it within 30 days of a deletion request, except where we must retain it for legal or security reasons.

7. Children

Paw Pantry is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. The service itself requires users to be 18 or older (per the platform it runs on).

8. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes will also be announced through the assistant.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hellopawpantry@gmail.com.
