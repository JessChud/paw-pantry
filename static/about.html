About Paw Pantry

Paw Pantry is a pet-supply planning project operated by Jessica Chudnovsky. The public website offers shopping checklists, a refill calculator, and a small catalog. A separate private prototype supports pet profiles and supply records; its Muse connector submission is under review.

How we prepare content

Our guides are AI-assisted original planning articles. They explain how to organize quantities, measurements, and shopping decisions. We have not physically tested the catalog products, and we do not present manufacturer statements as our own test results. We do not publish invented customer reviews, ratings, credentials, or personal pet-care experience.

Manufacturer links identify sources for the newer catalog entries. An entry is an example of a product type, not a claim that it is the best option or suitable for an individual animal. Check the current product label, specifications, price, and availability before buying. A veterinarian should guide medical, nutrition, allergy, and treatment decisions.

Affiliate links

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. We may earn a commission when a visitor chooses to buy through a labeled affiliate link. The website currently has one configured Amazon affiliate product link. Other catalog entries may have manufacturer information links or no purchase link. Manufacturer information links are not affiliate links. Chewy affiliate approval is pending.

Commission availability does not mean a product is recommended for your pet. We do not process payments or place orders. Retailers control checkout, shipping, returns, and product availability.

Corrections and contact

If you find an outdated specification, broken link, or unclear explanation, contact hellopawpantry@gmail.com and include the page address. Publication dates show when a guide was added; they do not imply continuous checks of retailer listings.
