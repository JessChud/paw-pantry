Pet-supply refill calculator

Estimate how long your existing supply will last using the amount remaining and your household’s actual daily use. Both amounts must use the same unit. This tool does not recommend a feeding portion.

Your entries stay in this page. They are not sent to Paw Pantry or saved when you leave. No reminder is scheduled and no order is placed. Check the actual supply and retailer delivery estimate.

Example

80 ounces remaining ÷ 2.5 ounces used daily = 32 days of supply. With five days allowed for shopping or delivery, consider a refill in 27 days. These numbers illustrate the calculation; they are not a feeding recommendation.
