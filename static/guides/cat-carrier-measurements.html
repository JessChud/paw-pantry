Measure for a cat carrier before comparing travel claims

Paw Pantry · Published September 19, 2026 · Planning guide

A carrier decision starts with your cat, the trip, and the exact model’s limits.

Write down measurements first

Use the measurement method provided by the carrier manufacturer and record your cat’s current weight. Compare those figures with the exact model and size, including usable interior space. An exterior measurement alone does not tell you how much room the animal has inside. If you are unsure about fit, ask the manufacturer or veterinarian before purchasing.

Check the trip separately

For a car trip, consider access, carrying handles, and how the carrier can be secured according to its instructions. For a flight, check the airline’s current requirements for your specific booking, including under-seat limits and any pet reservation requirements. A product described as airline-approved is not a promise that every airline, aircraft, or itinerary will accept it.

Compare everyday handling

Check where the openings are, how the closures work, and whether the liner is removable and washable. Measure the storage space at home too. A carrier that folds compactly may still need to be set up in advance of a trip; a rigid model may need a permanent place in a cupboard.

Keep a travel record

Save the carrier model, size, instructions, and retailer return terms. Check condition and fit again before travel, particularly for a growing kitten. The Sherpa example in our catalog is a starting point for examining manufacturer information, not a fit certification. Reconfirm the current listing and travel rules before deciding to buy.
