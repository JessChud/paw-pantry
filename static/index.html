About Paw Pantry

The public connector matches requests against broad product-type coverage and a smaller verified product catalog, then provides stateless shopping options and refill estimates. It cannot read or change the private Paw Pantry workspace. It does not schedule reminders, place orders, compare live prices, or track delivery.

Example requests: “Find a durable chew toy for my puppy.” “Estimate when this 10-pound bag will run out at 8 ounces per day.” “Show me dog toys in the catalog.”
