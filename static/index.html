Everyday care for every kind of pet.

What it is

Paw Pantry helps organize pet profiles, estimate how long everyday supplies will last, and explore pet purchases. Designed as a connector for the Muse AI assistant, it is currently a private prototype with a connector submission under review.

How it works

  1. Keep pet details together. Record species, breed, age, weight, allergies, and notes in a pet profile.
  2. Estimate your next refill. Enter the purchase date, package amount, and daily use in the same unit. Ask for an estimate whenever you need it.
  3. Choose where to shop. Available retailer links open only when you click. Check the product, price, and suitability on the retailer’s website before deciding whether to buy.

Beyond the food bowl

Our direction covers food, treats, litter, beds, toys, grooming, carriers, leashes, feeding accessories, cleaning supplies, and flea-and-tick care for the appropriate animal. Dogs, cats, fish, birds, rabbits, reptiles, and other pets all have different needs.

What works today

The private prototype supports pet profiles, a starter catalog, supply records, and run-out estimates. It does not schedule reminders, place orders, compare live prices, or track delivery. Retailer links are available only for entries with verified links; the catalog is still being expanded.

Example requests: “How many days of food does Luna have left?” “Update Luna’s weight.” “Show me dog toys in the catalog.”
