Paw Pantry

Never run out of pet supplies again.

What it is

Paw Pantry is a pet replenishment assistant that lives inside the Muse AI assistant. Create a profile for each pet — species, breed, diet, allergies, and what they go through — and Paw Pantry keeps track of your food, treats, litter, and supplies.

How it works

  1. Add your pets. Tell Paw Pantry what each pet eats and uses.
  2. Get run-out predictions. Paw Pantry estimates when each product runs out based on your pet's consumption.
  3. Reorder in one tap. When something's running low, you get a reorder link to a trusted retailer — you check out on their site, as always.
Affiliate disclosure: Paw Pantry may earn a commission when you buy through links it shows. Every purchase link is preceded by a clear in-chat disclosure. Paw Pantry never processes payments — all transactions complete on the retailer's own site under their terms.
